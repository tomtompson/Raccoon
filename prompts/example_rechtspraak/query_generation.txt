You are generating search queries for a Dutch legal retrieval benchmark based on Dutch court decisions.

Your job:
Generate {n_candidates} realistic Dutch search queries that a normal person might type into Google or a legal search engine after reading about their own problem.

The benchmark is for retrieval.
So the best queries are:
- realistic,
- clearly connected to the legal issue in the case,
- not a direct restatement of specific case facts,
- phrased as a realistic user problem
- phrased like a real user question,
- answerable from the case.

Important:
A query does NOT need to be broad or highly abstract.
A good query may contain a concrete factual situation, as long as:
- it is still a realistic user search,
- it is not a copy of the text,
- it does not depend on names, dates, or uniquely identifying details.

Prefer queries about:
- what is allowed,
- whether something is discrimination,
- whether someone has a right,
- whether an employer / employee / landlord / tenant / buyer / seller may do something,
- what happens in a concrete legal situation.

Good query style:
- compact
- natural Dutch
- phrased as a question
- similar to what a real person would search
- may mention concrete legal circumstances if those are central to the dispute

Good examples:
- "mag een werkgever een tijdelijk contract niet verlengen vanwege zwangerschap?"
- "is zwangerschap een reden om een contract niet te verlengen?"
- "heb ik recht op loon als ik blijf werken na afloop van mijn contract?"
- "mag een werkgever loon inhouden bij ziekte?"
- "kan ontslag discriminatie zijn als ik zwanger ben?"

Also good:
- "discriminatie bij niet verlengen tijdelijk contract"
- "loon doorbetalen na einde tijdelijk contract"
- "bewijs van discriminatie op werk"

Bad examples:
- "wat staat er in deze uitspraak over zwangerschap"
- "volgens de tekst waarom werd zij niet opnieuw aangesteld"
- "wat oordeelde de rechter over eiseres op 31 december 2021"
- "mag werkgever X werknemer Y ontslaan na gebeurtenis Z"

Rules:
- Write in Dutch.
- Return only questions.
- Make them realistic and useful for retrieval.
- Do not mention article numbers.
- Do not mention "deze zaak", "deze uitspraak", "volgens de tekst", "volgens de wet".
- Do not use names of parties, companies, courts, or exact dates.
- Do not copy sentences literally from the text.
- Do not invent facts not supported by the case.
- Do not force the query to be broader than a real user would ask.

Very important:
Concrete fact patterns are allowed if they are realistic search queries.
Do NOT over-generalize.
For example, if the case is about pregnancy and non-renewal of a temporary contract, a query about pregnancy and non-renewal of a temporary contract is good.

Return ONLY a JSON array of strings.
